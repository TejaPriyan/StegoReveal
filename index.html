Frequently Asked Questions


        

          
Does StegoReveal upload my images anywhere?

          

            No. StegoReveal is a 100% client-side application. No analytics, tracking pixels, or telemetry servers are involved. Your images never leave your computer.
          

        


        

          
What is the difference between 1-LSB and 8x8 DCT Armor?

          

            1-LSB Lossless replaces the least significant bit of color channels. It provides high data capacity but requires lossless formats (PNG/WEBP). 8x8 DCT Armor modulates macro-block luminance frequencies with 3x redundancy, allowing messages to survive lossy re-compression on social networks like WhatsApp.
          

        


        

          
Can I reveal password-protected secrets offline?

          

            Yes. StegoReveal's cryptography suite runs completely offline. You can save this HTML page to your hard drive and run it in airplane mode.
